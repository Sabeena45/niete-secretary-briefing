Numbers and definitions.

Schools and teachers: 415 programme schools in six sectors (about 341 primary, the rest middle and secondary only). 6,354 teachers: 3,261 primary, 2,979 middle and secondary only, 114 level not recorded. Counts accounts; a few people hold two.

Visits and AI coach: 5,884 lessons observed by coaches since Aug 2026 (5,761 reviewed, 5,530 debriefed), 3,084 teachers. 29,766 recorded lessons coached by Rumi, 2,744 teachers, Jul to 19 Nov.

Scores: FICO rubric v3, 8 Sep to 19 Nov: 3,716 coach visits, average 65%; 24,622 AI-scored lessons, average 63%. Two newer coach forms are left out.

Quizzes: 27,733 children, 22,770 finished one, 1,065 teachers, to 18 Nov. First-try average 78% (lessons), 72% (videos).

Training: NIETE levels only. 1,921 teachers hold all four; 3,764 hold at least one. Middle and secondary teachers are assigned levels 3 and 4.

Tests on a phone: 177 children finished, 58 schools, median 18.6 min. Assessor time (about 21 min) is from the Rawalpindi study, May 2026. The AI scoring cost is an estimate.

Source: governed NIETE data, rules v0.42.2, test accounts left out. Coaches: 55 in the plan; 70 coach accounts exist, including mentors.
